Altair is more than eighty years old, even though the name is new.

In 1945, Kirtilal Jhaveri, along with his partners Jormal and Ratilal Jhaveri, began a small firm trading polished diamonds after moving to Mumbai from the village of Palanpur in Gujarat. The business grew, and before long their sons joined and took it into the cutting and polishing of rough. Real growth came in the late 1950s, when the firm began exporting to markets such as Japan and Israel.

Working with Japanese buyers taught the firm a great deal about precision and quality in diamond cutting and polishing. Inventory was separated into groups called assortments, and each assortment had a strict set of criteria for the cut, colour, and clarity of its diamonds. That pursuit of perfection was the foundation of J. Ratilal & Co. then, and it remains the foundation of Altair today.

The third generation joined in the 1970s. Cutting and polishing had to stop in the late 1980s, but the trading side flourished and opened new markets, mainly the USA and Canada.

The fourth generation came in through the late 2000s and early 2010s, and set about restarting manufacturing, this time in large polished diamonds, a carat and above. They learned the craft from rough through to final faceting. It is the reason Altair can judge a stone on how it actually performs and not only on what the certificate says.

For eighty years the work was the same. Understand what a buyer needed, then find the diamonds that answered it. Altair does that one client at a time now, and the promise has not changed: you will always receive more than you expect, and never anything less.
